FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Tradeva really free?
Yes. Every feature on this page is free to use, with no trial period and no card required at sign-up.
Can I track multiple trading accounts?
Yes — personal, prop firm, demo — each with its own balance, trades and statistics. Switching accounts updates every page instantly.
Does it work on mobile?
Tradeva is fully responsive and built mobile-first for logging. Add a trade from your phone at the desk, then review it properly on a laptop later.
Can I upload screenshots of my trades?
Yes — an entry and an exit image per trade. They're compressed in your browser before upload, so charts stay readable without eating your data.
Can I export my reports?
The Profit Calendar exports as a PNG or a branded monthly report, ready to share or file with your own records.
Is my data secure?
Your trades live in your own account and are only readable by you. Sign-in supports email with verification or Google, and logging out clears cached data from that device.
Can I really track trading psychology?
Yes, and it's a core part of the product rather than a text box. Mood, energy and stress are logged as structured data, so they can be compared against results over time.
Does it support Forex?
Yes. Pairs, sessions, pips and R multiples are first-class fields, and the planner ships with the major FX pairs built in.
Does it support Crypto and indices?
Yes. Tradeva is instrument-agnostic — if you can record an entry, an exit and a risk, you can journal it.
Can I use it for a prop firm account?
Yes, and it's a common use. Set the account's starting balance, then use the planner's max drawdown and trade limits to stay inside your firm's rules.
